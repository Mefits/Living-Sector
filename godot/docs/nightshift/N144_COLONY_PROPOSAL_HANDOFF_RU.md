Covers continuation of earliest active colony site, passenger versus materials decision, treasury/subsidy and workforce budget, outstanding-contract suppression, resource-bearing planet selection, physical registry reachability, tech gating, metals availability/quote, passenger/carrier/builder readiness, orbital rendezvous, same-system preference, gas shortage bonus and deterministic score/tie-break.

No guessed defaults for required economic values; fail-closed if construction constants absent. Markets must include an externally validated `metals_quote` and `quote_valid` from `sourcing::purchase_cost`, not naive price multiplication. Ships must supply true `passenger_capacity` from `ship_class`, and `builder_capable` from `construction::executor::capable`. `distances` must be physical `WorldRegistry.shortest_distance` (N142), **not** gate-aware N59.

**Boundaries:** N79 handles admission; N118 handles site creation/commission and prepaid contract issuance; N92 aftercare; N125–N127 population contracts/production. N144 neither mutates world nor creates stock/credits, posts contracts or launches a ship. A proposal with reason other than `ready` is diagnostic only. Existing Rust tie-break on score uses f32 total_cmp; this port uses float comparisons, so exact NaN/-0 and f32 rounding parity remain unverified. Saturating i64 operations are not replicated for extreme balances. `active.remaining` and `labor_required/completed` must be sourced from the authoritative ConstructionSite. The `outstanding` field must be derived from native `strategy::outstanding` predicate.
